🛡️ Hybrid Athlete Cardio Rules

  • • Running + Leg Day: Never run 5-10km on Heavy Squat Day (Day 3). Swap for light cycling to prevent knee injuries.
  • • Best Running Days: Day 4 (Pull B) and Day 6 (Legs B / Hamstrings). Back and bicep training does not drain your running legs!
  • • Cycling Benefit: Zero eccentric load. Whenever joints or shins feel tight from running, replace running with cycling 1:3 ratio (e.g., 5km run = 15km cycling).
